# Product review recording: proposed changes

**Status:** Product direction approved by Bruno on September 23, 2026. Implementation and verification are in progress; this document is not a deployment sign-off.

## Approval and implementation addendum

- **Approved giving model:** No new Olea-run grants or grant applications. Name the benefit **Olea's Circle of Generosity** and state that **15% of profits flow back to nonprofits as unrestricted donations**. Keep the separate customer-owned Grant Platform and historical records. Do not imply every member receives a donation.
- **Approved public referral headline:** **10% of the first successful quarterly or annual membership payment, capped at CAD $500 once per referred organization**. An introduction, demo, trial, or renewal alone earns nothing. The local worktree replaces both legacy payout paths with a pending, Stripe-invoice-backed commission; it has not been deployed. Refund/chargeback treatment and payout operations still need finance approval.
- **Approved navigation:** Remove Templates and Consulting from the member sidebar and global search, but retain their deep links and historical data. Remove the separate Olea grants entry while keeping Grant Platform.
- **Approved content and layout:** Remove fixed hourly/service-package prices, per-plan founding-price calculations, old grant claims, and sponsor explanatory cards. Retain the founding code, regular plan prices, and discount flow. Add concise community conduct guidance.
- **Directory privacy remains unresolved:** No member contact directory is published by this approval. Confirm audience, opt-in, and which organization/person fields may be shown before implementation.
- **Local verification so far:** Referral application, approval, unique link, repeated approval, dashboard clipboard copy, signup redirect, revocation, and invalid-link cleanup passed in a local authenticated Playwright flow. The referral lifecycle passed three repeat runs; the focused cross-flow Chromium suite passed 29/29 tests. Public pricing EN/FR, signup legal consent, team invitation acceptance, and authenticated locale switching also passed locally. Staging and payment-ledger verification remain outstanding.

## Decisions to approve first

1. **Olea Gives model:** Confirm that Olea Gives is *no longer an application-based grant program*. Rosalyn describes donating **15% of Olive Social Impact's profit, after overhead**, as unrestricted donations to nonprofits, rather than granting a fixed amount or percentage of revenue [01:24-02:20]. This is materially different from the current site, dashboard, and `/grants` workflow. Finance/legal should approve the exact public wording and donation-selection process before publication. Do not assume every member receives a donation.
2. **Referral payout model:** Resolved after the review: 10% of the actual first successful quarterly or annual membership payment, capped at CAD $500 once per referred organization. The old $100 demo plus $400 retained split is retired for new referrals. Finance still needs to approve what happens on refunds/chargebacks and how payouts are disbursed. The separate grant-writing partner arrangement is not the public member referral program.
3. **Founding offer:** Remove the *per-plan "Founding Year 1" price row* [03:50-04:22], but keep the approved 15%-off code and first-50-organizations eligibility unless you explicitly decide otherwise. The conversation says to replace the tier-card calculation with the code, not to abandon the code. Confirm whether the founding-offer notice should remain on the pricing page.
4. **Service discounts:** Quote-only coaching, admin support, and retreats were agreed [13:48-18:36], while Canopy/Harvest still advertise 10% off coaching and admin packages. Confirm the discount base, exclusions, and whether Harvest's "board retreat facilitation support" is an included benefit or merely a discounted/custom add-on.
5. **Directory visibility:** Decide whether the proposed member/sponsor directory is visible to all paying members, whether individuals or organizations appear, what contact data is public, and whether explicit opt-in is required [46:56-47:20, 49:49-53:00]. Do not expose team member email addresses by default.
