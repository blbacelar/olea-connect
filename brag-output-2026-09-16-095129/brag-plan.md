# Brag Plan: Olea Connects™

## What is this app?
Olea Connects™ is a bilingual governance and reporting home base that helps Canadian nonprofit teams move from setup to branded, board-ready work.

## The angle
The video treats governance as living infrastructure rather than paperwork. The Olea tree becomes the organizing visual: one brand profile grows into a connected workspace for board operations, KPI reporting, funding, and community. The edit stays specific to the real product and lets the interface prove the claim.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The Olea tree mark draws on over a warm cream field while the line “Governance, branded.” resolves beside it. A fine branch line grows into the next scene.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A real Olea-style workspace assembles with the dark-green sidebar, Maple Grove Community House identity, and branded dashboard cards.
- A visible cursor confirms the brand profile, then the organization colours flow into a board-ready document preview.
- Board Calendar, KPI Dashboard, Grant Platform, and Community modules arrive as connected work surfaces rather than a generic feature grid.

## Outro / punchline
“One login. Your brand. Practical support.” resolves above the Olea Connects™ lockup and “Built for nonprofit organizations.”

## User flow worth showing
Set up the organization brand → open a connected nonprofit workspace → produce board-ready reporting and access funding/community support.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a warm, confident social-enterprise product film
- Interpretation: restrained copy, longer settled holds, purposeful camera movement, and tactile interface motion with no hype language.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: begin at low volume, let the workspace reveal carry slightly more presence, then soften under the final lockup with a short fade.
- Music cue guidance: bundled 109.96 BPM preset. Land major reveals at 8.74s, 13.11s, and 17.47s. Use the 9.29s/10.37s/11.46s beat window for the three workflow states while preserving readable holds.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use bass/RMS data to breathe the tree canopy and warm workspace glow by roughly 3-5%. No waveform or visualizer graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse; one soft reveal, one quiet UI click, and one restrained final bell.
- Audio-coupled moments: logo draw, cursor confirmation, module sequence, final lockup.
- Restraint rule: sound must support trust and clarity, never feel like a corporate jingle or compete with readable copy.
